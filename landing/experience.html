About this experience

Amsterdam is best understood from a bike saddle — and best of all on the canals that tourists never find. Lars has spent 12 years cycling every alley, bridge, and hidden hofje in the Jordaan, and this route is the distillation of everything he's discovered.

You'll leave the main ring canals behind within the first ten minutes, weaving into a quieter city of moss-covered bridges, courtyard gardens, and local brown cafés where the menu is still written in chalk. No headsets, no crowds — just Lars, your group, and three hours of the Amsterdam that most visitors never reach.

What you'll do

  • 01
    Meet at Westerkerk
    Lars will be waiting with bikes and helmets at the church entrance. Everyone gets fitted and briefed in five minutes flat.
  • 02
    The Jordaan's hidden hofjes
    Locked courtyards that only locals know the entrances to. Lars has access to two that have been here since the 17th century.
  • 03
    Seven forgotten canal bridges
    Each with its own story — the history of the city told through the oldest crossings on the lesser-known waterways.
  • 04
    Brown café break
    A 300-year-old café that doesn't serve tourists — just regulars and people lucky enough to know Lars. Snack included.
  • 05
    Return loop via Prinsengracht
    An evening canal view that'll make your photos look like you paid a professional to find it — which, in a sense, you did.
