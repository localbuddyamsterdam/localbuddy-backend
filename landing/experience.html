About this experience

Amsterdam is best understood from a bike saddle — and best of all on the canals that tourists never find. Lars has spent 12 years cycling every alley, bridge, and hidden hofje in the Jordaan, and this route is the distillation of everything he's discovered.

You'll leave the main ring canals behind within the first ten minutes, weaving into a quieter city of moss-covered bridges, courtyard gardens, and local brown cafés where the menu is still written in chalk. No headsets, no crowds — just Lars, your group, and three hours of the Amsterdam most visitors never reach.
